Privacy Policy

Adept Accountancy Group is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect your name, contact details, business information, financial records, tax information, identity verification details and messages you send to us.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to enquiries, provide accountancy and tax services, prepare and submit returns, maintain records, comply with legal obligations and manage our relationship with you.

3. Legal Basis

We process personal data where necessary for contract, legal obligation, legitimate interests and, where required, consent.

4. Data Storage and Security

We store information securely using reputable systems and take reasonable steps to protect personal data from unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

5. Sharing Information

Where necessary, we may share information with HMRC, Companies House, software providers, professional advisers, regulators and other parties where required to provide services or comply with the law.

6. Retention

We retain records only for as long as necessary for legal, regulatory, tax and business purposes.

7. Your Rights

You may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data.

8. Contact

For privacy queries, contact: info@adeptaccountancygroup.co.uk

9. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how your data is handled, you can contact us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.
